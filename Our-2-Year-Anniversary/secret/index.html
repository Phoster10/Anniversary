A Little Secret Thought About You ❤️

Yeah, Tracy… two years. Can you believe it? Aren't you happy that your first boyfriend is someone you've actually lasted this long with? 😂❤️

I know I am. It hasn't always been the easiest road getting here. We've had our arguments, our annoying moments, and times when we probably wondered why we chose each other in the first place. 😂 But honestly, I wouldn't change any of it.

Every good moment, every difficult moment, every argument and every laugh somehow helped me understand you more. It helped me learn who you really are, and somehow, through all of it, I just kept falling deeper and deeper in love with you.

And yes… you do annoy me A LOT. 😭 And I know I annoy you too. Sometimes you make me feel like I could just strangle you. 😂

But then I remember that underneath all that stubbornness is my girl who sometimes just wants a forehead kiss, a little rest, and to be reminded that she's beautiful until she finally believes it.

And that's the girl I love.

So, Tracy, after two years, through all the madness, laughter, arguments, love, and everything in between… I'm still here. Still choosing you. Still learning you. Still loving you.

And honestly?

I wouldn't want it any other way. ❤️

— Phoster
